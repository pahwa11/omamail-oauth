Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Omamail Personal is a desktop email client used by its owner to access their own Google account.

  • What it accesses: Gmail messages and Google Calendar events of the signed-in account, through Google's APIs, only to show and manage them on the owner's computer.
  • Where data goes: data travels directly between Google and the owner's computer. No server run by this app receives, stores or processes it.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's system keyring; a mail cache stays on that computer.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
